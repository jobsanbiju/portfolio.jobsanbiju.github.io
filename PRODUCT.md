# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

This is a personal filmmaker portfolio for Job San Biju. The audience is intentionally broad: commercial clients, creative agencies, directors, producers, collaborators, festival or program reviewers, and general viewers who want to understand the work.

Visitors may arrive with different levels of intent, from quickly judging the visual style to deciding whether to reach out for a project. Their main jobs are to watch selected work, understand Job's cinematic voice, find enough trust signals to evaluate fit, contact him for a project, and optionally follow on Instagram.

## Product Purpose

The site exists to make Job's filmmaking craft immediately legible through motion, image, tone, and selective written context. Success means a visitor quickly understands the work, feels the cinematic point of view, watches the selected film, and knows how to contact Job or follow his work.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, premium, intimate, raw, moody, human, experimental, elegant, and tactile.

The tone should be confident without feeling corporate, emotional without becoming sentimental, and polished without losing the personal, handmade quality of the current direction.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site feel like a generic dark neon portfolio, corporate template layout, stock creative-agency site, or repetitive card-heavy design.

Avoid visual effects that feel decorative for their own sake. Glow, grain, halation, overlays, and motion should feel motivated by filmmaking and optics.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with moving image and mood. The first viewport should immediately communicate cinematic craft.
2. Preserve the creator-led direction while improving polish, spacing, hierarchy, and responsiveness.
3. Make work easy to watch. The path from landing to selected films should be obvious and low-friction.
4. Make contact easy without overexplaining. Clients and collaborators should quickly find email, Instagram, and the reason to reach out.
5. Use atmosphere with restraint. Halation, glow, overlays, and motion should support readability and tone, never compete with the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use solid baseline accessibility: keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, sufficient text contrast, reduced-motion support, touch-friendly mobile controls, and graceful media fallbacks. The site should remain understandable if video playback fails or motion is reduced.
